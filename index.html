About

UX Researcher & Cognitive Neuroscientist.

  • B.A.Sc. Arts & Science, Psychology, Neuroscience & Behaviour
  • PhD Psychology (Cognitive Neuroscience specialization)

My journey began in cognitive neuroscience, where I explored how the brain develops and adapts using cognitive, clinical, neuroimaging, and genetic data. Through years of research, I found a drive for asking meaningful questions 🤔, making sense of complex data 📈, and turning insights into impact 🌎. This path naturally led me to a strong interest in user research and applied research 🔍 in industry—where an understanding of the mind remains at the core.

Along the way, my PhD gave me more than subject expertise—it trained me to think critically and creatively 🧠 , learn new domains of knowledge with ease, curiosity, and enthusiasm 📚, manage complex projects from start to finish 📆, and navigate ambiguity with clarity, care, and excitement📍. I developed the ability to synthesize large volumes of information 📊, clearly communicate insights to diverse audiences 👩‍🏫, and lead with empathy, curiosity, and precision 💖.

Dance has always provided me with a sense of calmness and joy! Over the years, I’ve explored a range of dance styles, from the grace of ballet, the bold energy of hip-hop to the expressive storytelling of Bollywood. Most recently, I’ve immersed myself in Kathak, a classical Indian dance form that weaves together intricate footwork, rhythmic patterns, and expressive gestures.

What I love the most about Kathak is the balance that it demands - it’s both structured and fluid, precise yet poetic. Every spin, every beat, every gaze requires deep concentration and control. It challenges me to stay grounded in rhythm while allowing emotion to flow freely—a beautiful juxtaposition of discipline and artistry that I find deeply meditative and energizing!
